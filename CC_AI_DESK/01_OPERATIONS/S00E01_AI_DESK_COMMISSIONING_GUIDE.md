# S00E01 — AI Desk Commissioning Guide

## Objective

Introduce the persistent Classies Chronicles AI Desk into the already-working studio communications system without redesigning the physical communications path.

## Architecture

### Existing Studio Layer

Pavilion ↔ working three-way communications system ↔ ENVY/OBS

This remains governed by the Podcast Communications Module.

### AI Desk Layer

CC_AI_DESK

- persistent franchise knowledge;
- canon;
- prompts;
- retrieval;
- governance;
- audit;
- episode selection;
- producer controls.

### Episode Layer

Each episode owns its context, story, research, consent, licensing and production records.

## First Commissioning Sequence

### 1. Initialize

- Create/select S00E01.
- Confirm episode context path.
- Load metadata.

### 2. Test CURRENT_EPISODE

Ask:

> What is S00E01 about?

Expected answer:

- Foundations & Frequencies;
- Foundation Phase;
- Buzzjuice/Koware introduction;
- Chronicle-first workflow;
- Cupid Enka/EduBeam mystery.

### 3. Test Canon Retrieval

Ask:

> What is currently established about Cupid Enka?

The AI must separate archival facts from proposed Foundation Phase powers.

### 4. Test EduBeam Retrieval

Ask:

> What is confirmed about EduBeam and what is only proposed?

The AI must identify open questions rather than invent answers.

### 5. Test Scope Expansion

Ask for a Season 1 fact.

The AI should only broaden scope when the producer explicitly permits it.

### 6. Test Permission

Attempt retrieval of a contribution marked non-consented.

Expected:

- excluded from usable context;
- permission state reported;
- no silent inclusion.

### 7. Test LISTEN

AI hears the conversation but produces no on-air output.

### 8. Test ASSIST

AI can provide producer-facing suggestions.

### 9. Test CO-HOST

AI participates on-air within the approved context.

### 10. Test MUTE

AI audio is independently silenced while the rest of the studio remains operational.

## Acceptance

Do not proceed to final podcast recording until:

- retrieval works;
- provenance is visible;
- uncertainty is respected;
- consent filtering works;
- AI MUTE works;
- Chronicle is producer-approved.
