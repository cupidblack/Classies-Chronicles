# Episode Generation — Step-by-Step Operating Guide

## Step 0 — Create episode folder

Create the standard episode archive before generating story material.

## Step 1 — Complete metadata

Fill identity, dates, locations, target durations, participants and production status.

## Step 2 — Load season/chapter notes

Attach season notes and the chapter key factor.

## Step 3 — Load previous episode

Attach the previous episode's final context, approved Chronicle and continuity references.

## Step 4 — Load canonical sources

Attach:
- character canon;
- technology canon;
- relevant archive;
- Bible reference;
- course reference;
- verified news;
- consent/licensing state.

## Step 5 — Run continuity preflight

Ask the AI Desk to identify:
- direct continuity;
- unresolved conflicts;
- possible spoilers;
- claims that are not supported.

## Step 6 — Build the story contract

Define:
- main topic;
- central question;
- theme;
- emotional arc;
- humour;
- mystery;
- ending hook.

## Step 7 — Generate story outline

Do not generate the final Chronicle yet.

## Step 8 — Producer review

Approve, revise or reject the outline.

## Step 9 — Generate Chronicle package

Generate the 200-second script, scene list, visual plan and audio plan.

## Step 10 — Canon audit

List every new claim and classify it.

## Step 11 — Producer Chronicle approval

No approval = no trailer and no final podcast recording.

## Step 12 — Produce Chronicle

Create visuals, narration, sound and final edit.

## Step 13 — Derive trailer

Extract the strongest 20 seconds from the completed Chronicle.

## Step 14 — Build podcast

Use the standard podcast structure.

## Step 15 — Record

Use the existing ENVY/Pavilion communications setup. Do not redesign audio routing as part of episode generation.

## Step 16 — Edit and QA

Check:
- audio;
- video;
- continuity;
- captions;
- factual news;
- Bible reference;
- consent;
- licensing;
- AI behaviour.

## Step 17 — Freeze archive

Store final context, provenance, approval state, transcript, media references and changelog.

## Step 18 — Post-episode analysis

Record:
- what worked;
- what failed;
- audience response;
- AI observations;
- retrieval problems;
- persona proposals.

## Step 19 — Prepare next episode

Only after the episode is archived should S00E02 context be created.
