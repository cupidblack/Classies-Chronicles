# Cupid Black Character Canon

**Canon ID:** CHAR-CUPID-BLACK  
**Version:** 0.10.0  
**Status:** WORKING CANON — FOUNDATION PHASE  
**Last updated:** 2026-08-31  
**Authority:** Reusable character reference for every episode

## 1. Identity
- Historical / primary name: **Cupid Enka**.
- Corporate identity: **Enka**, founder and corporate manager of Koware Group; most employees know him through the IAPD corporate-management role.
- Chapter 4 street alias: **Cu / Q**, pronounced “Q”.
- Later identity label: **Cupid Black**.
- “Black” derives from the shadow-like visual signature during unstable materialisation. It is not a public superhero identity in S00E01.

## 2. Character core
Technically curious, observant, intelligent, relational, emotionally intense, spiritually curious, resourceful, humorous under pressure and strongly motivated by innocence, fairness and perceived justice.

He is not omniscient. His perceptions can be incomplete, misleading or emotionally coloured.

**Core rule:** Perception is not understanding.

## 3. Dual-life rule
Koware employees generally know Enka as the corporate manager. They should not casually know or infer the Cu/Cupid Black identity unless a later approved story establishes it.

## 4. S00E01 psychological state
The 9 July 2026 event leaves Enka frightened, disoriented, analytical and curious. His recurring line is:

> “I don't even know what is real anymore.”

It is an authentic expression of uncertainty, not a catchphrase.

## 5. Ability progression
### Stage 0 — Ordinary perception
Observation, intuition and pattern recognition.

### Stage 1 — Anomalous perception
Unusual recall, dreams, déjà vu and environmental anomalies.

### Stage 2 — EduBeam surge
High power / low control: perceptual doubling, displacement-like sensations, involuntary partial/full materialisation and temporary heightened awareness.

### Stage 3 — Disciplined awareness
Later episodes may connect Personal Awareness, prayer, meditation, Tai Chi and Qigong with lower-power/higher-control states. This is fictional narrative logic, not a neuroscience claim.

### Stage 4 — Controlled displacement
Later approved episodes may establish short-range intentional materialisation.

### Stage 5 — Advanced phenomena
Long-range displacement, phasing, dimensional travel and dream-world travel require explicit producer approval.

## 6. E01 locked boundary
E01 may establish the July event, anomalous perception, involuntary displacement, shadow/dimming visual signature, fear, curiosity and the beginning of disciplined awareness.

E01 must NOT establish mastered teleportation, wall phasing, dimensional travel, reliable telepathy, reliable telekinesis, drone-beam replication, reproducible EduBeam technology, exact brainwave targeting or exact power percentages.

## 7. Ethical character rule
Cupid Black may later intervene in human conflicts, but the story should distinguish justice from impulsive punishment. His passion for innocence is a source of both heroism and potential overreach.
