# Cupid Black Character Canon

**Canon ID:** CHAR-CUPID-BLACK  
**Version:** 0.6.0  
**Status:** WORKING CANON — FOUNDATION PHASE  
**Single reusable character reference:** YES  
**Last updated:** 2026-08-31

## 1. Identity

**Historical/primary name:** Cupid Enka  
**Later identity:** Cupid Black  
**Alias:** Cu / "Q" (pronounced "Q")

"Cupid Black" and "Cu" are later identity labels. Do not casually insert them into earlier chronology.

## 2. Core characteristics

Enka is:

- technically curious;
- observant;
- intelligent;
- emotionally intense;
- relational;
- humorous under pressure;
- motivated by innocence and perceived justice;
- willing to improvise;
- spiritually curious;
- increasingly interested in disciplined awareness;
- capable of helping strangers despite personal risk.

He is not omniscient, infallible or automatically heroic.

## 3. Critical rule

**Perception is not understanding.**

He may perceive a signal, emotion, possible future or anomalous location without knowing its cause or reliability.

His abilities must never become an automatic problem-solving engine.

## 4. Ability progression

### Stage 0 — Ordinary perception
Technically observant and unusually attentive.

### Stage 1 — Anomalous perception
Dreams, déjà vu, unusual recall and environmental anomalies become increasingly noticeable.

### Stage 2 — EduBeam surge
A strong exposure can produce high-power, low-control effects.

### Stage 3 — Controlled awareness
Later story material may connect prayer, Personal Awareness, meditation, Tai Chi and Qigong with weaker, more controllable states.

### Stage 4 — Controlled displacement
Short-range materialization may become possible after substantial development.

### Stage 5 — Advanced phenomena
Long-range materialization, phasing and dimensional/dream-world travel remain future story material.

## 5. Power rule

**EduBeam = high power / low control**  
**Awareness practice = lower power / higher control**

Effects dissipate and vary according to fictional story conditions.

## 6. Materialization signature

When partial materialization occurs:

- Enka may appear darker than normal;
- the environment may subtly dim;
- the form can resemble a shadow, dust or visual distortion;
- darkness becomes more defined as materialization increases;
- normal colour returns during stabilization.

This is the proposed visual origin of "Black."

## 7. Ethical limitation

Enka wants innocence protected and wrongdoing confronted. Because his information is incomplete, he may intervene too aggressively.

**Recurring moral question:**  
"If you can perceive something others cannot perceive, do you have the right to intervene?"

## 8. Training

Future story material may include awareness practice, meditation, prayer, Tai Chi, Qigong, sound/frequency experiments and fictional technology experiments.

Dangerous electrical or neurological experimentation must never be presented as actionable real-world instruction.

## 9. S00E01 boundary

E01 may show:

- EduBeam exposure;
- dual-location perception;
- involuntary displacement;
- partial/full materialization as established by Chapter 4;
- darker visual signature;
- uncertainty.

E01 must not show mastery or fully explain the powers.

## 10. Signature line

Use sparingly.
