# EduBeam Technology Canon

**Canon ID:** TECH-EDUBEAM  
**Version:** 0.9.0  
**Status:** WORKING CANON — FOUNDATION PHASE  
**Approval:** Producer review pending  
**Last updated:** 2026-08-31

## 1. Fictional status

EduBeam is a **fictional** learning / neurological / remote-beam phenomenon in the Chronicles universe.

It is not real technology and must never be presented as real science.

## 2. Established narrative facts

- EduBeam is associated with a satellite/remote-beam phenomenon.
- Enka experienced it before the Foundation Phase.
- The 9 July 2026 Singapore event is an established archive event.
- Strong exposure can produce abnormal perception and displacement-like effects.
- Effects dissipate rather than remaining permanently at maximum intensity.

## 3. Unknowns

Unless an approved archive establishes otherwise:
- creator;
- owner;
- engineering design;
- controller/operator;
- ultimate purpose;
- why Enka is affected;
- whether the system is adaptive, experimental or malfunctioning.

## 4. Fictional targeting hierarchy

Possible future story logic:
1. active network/location signal;
2. recent network activity;
3. last-known digital transaction/location;
4. expected geographic location;
5. broad-area targeting when precision fails.

Exact accuracy figures are undefined.

## 5. Cognitive targeting

A fictional neural/brainwave signature may later explain why the system can distinguish Enka.

This is **PROPOSED_NEW_CANON**, not real neuroscience.

## 6. Event classes

- Routine/random scan — lower-intensity fictional event.
- Processing-cycle event — potentially stronger event associated with system processing.
- Precision lock — concentrated event directed toward Enka's current or expected location.

Do not hard-code a twice-yearly schedule or numerical percentages until a producer-approved episode requires them.

## 7. Precision failure

When precise location is unavailable, a broader fictional beam may occur.

It can support fictional:
- confused perception;
- unusual local experiences;
- mistaken identity;
- environmental anomalies.

### Editorial safety rule

Never use EduBeam to explain real-world missing persons, deaths, disasters or crimes as though the fiction were factual.

## 8. Effect progression

Possible story effects:
- enhanced recall;
- perceptual distortion;
- dual-location sensation;
- lucid/dream-like states;
- temporary displacement;
- partial materialisation;
- full materialisation;
- phasing;
- unusual awareness.

These vary by event and Enka's condition.

## 9. Central contrast

> **EduBeam = high power / low control**

> **Awareness practice = lower power / higher control**

This is the long-term arc, not a claim about real neuroscience.

## 10. S00E01 treatment

E01 reconstructs the established 9 July Singapore event as a mystery. The audience experiences the phenomenon before receiving any complete explanation.
