# EduBeam Technology Canon

**Canon ID:** TECH-EDUBEAM  
**Version:** 0.10.0  
**Status:** WORKING CANON — FOUNDATION PHASE  
**Last updated:** 2026-08-31

## 1. Fictional status
EduBeam is a fictional learning / neurological / remote-beam phenomenon in the Chronicles universe. It is not real technology and must never be presented as real science.

## 2. Established narrative facts
- EduBeam is associated with a satellite/remote-beam phenomenon.
- Enka experienced it before the Foundation Phase.
- The 9 July 2026 Singapore event is an approved archive event.
- Strong exposure can produce abnormal perception and displacement-like effects.
- Effects dissipate rather than remaining permanently at maximum intensity.

## 3. Unknowns
Creator, owner, engineering design, controller/operator, ultimate purpose, reason Enka is affected, and whether the system is adaptive/experimental/malfunctioning remain unknown unless later canon approves them.

## 4. Fictional targeting model
Possible future logic, in descending order of precision:
1. active network/location signal;
2. recent network activity;
3. last-known digital activity/location;
4. expected geographic location;
5. broad-area targeting when precision fails.

No exact accuracy figures are canon.

## 5. Cognitive signature
A fictional neural signature may later explain why the system can distinguish Enka. This is **PROPOSED_NEW_CANON** and not real neuroscience.

## 6. Event classes
- Routine/random scan: lower-intensity fictional event.
- Processing-cycle event: potentially stronger event associated with system processing.
- Precision lock: concentrated event directed toward Enka's current or expected location.

The producer may later establish a seasonal cadence, but exact twice-yearly timing and numerical intensity percentages remain uncommitted.

## 7. Precision failure
Broad targeting can produce fictional local anomalies such as confused perception, mistaken identity and environmental disturbances.

**Editorial safety rule:** Never use EduBeam to explain real-world missing persons, deaths, disasters or crimes as though the fiction were factual.

## 8. Visual effect
Partial materialisation begins as a darker/shadow-like intrusion. The surrounding room subtly dims; dust-like edges or a clouded-light effect can accompany the transition. Colour returns as the figure stabilises. Full materialisation removes the original body from its starting point.

## 9. Long-term contrast
**EduBeam = high power / low control.**  
**Awareness practice = lower power / higher control.**

This is a fictional story engine, not a scientific claim.

## 10. E01 treatment
The audience experiences the phenomenon before receiving a complete explanation. Evidence is shown; explanation remains provisional.

## 11. Canon classification
**ARCHIVAL / CONFIRMED:** 9 July Singapore event; humming/flickering; Enka recognises EduBeam; later materialisation near a colleague's apartment; continuing after-effects in Chapter 4.

**PROPOSED_NEW_CANON:** dual-location subjective experience; shadow-like transition; possible targeting hierarchy; future relationship between disciplined awareness and controlled phenomena.

**UNKNOWN:** mechanism; operator; why Enka is affected; intentionality of tracking.
