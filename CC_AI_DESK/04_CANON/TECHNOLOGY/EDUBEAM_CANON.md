## 1. Fictional status

EduBeam is a **fictional** learning / neurological / remote-beam technology in the Chronicles universe.

It is not a real scientific technology and must never be presented as one.

## 2. Established narrative facts

- EduBeam is associated with a satellite / remote-beam phenomenon.
- Enka experienced it before the Foundation Phase.
- The 9 July 2026 Singapore event is an established archive event.
- Strong exposure can produce abnormal perception and displacement-like effects.
- Effects dissipate rather than remaining permanently at maximum intensity.

## 3. Unknowns

Unless approved archive evidence establishes otherwise:
- creator is unknown;
- ownership is unknown;
- engineering design is unknown;
- actual purpose is unknown;
- controller/operator is unknown;
- ultimate objective is unknown;
- whether it is adaptive, experimental or malfunctioning is unknown;
- why Enka is affected is unknown.

## 4. Fictional targeting hierarchy

Possible future story logic:

1. active network/location signal;
2. recent network activity;
3. last-known digital transaction/location;
4. expected geographic location;
5. broader-area targeting when precise targeting fails.

Exact accuracy figures remain undefined.

## 5. Cognitive targeting

A fictional neural/brainwave signature associated with Enka may later explain why the system can distinguish him.

This is **PROPOSED_NEW_CANON**, not real science.

## 6. Event classes

### Routine/random scan
Lower-intensity event with subtle temporary effects.

### Processing-cycle event
Potentially stronger event associated with major system processing periods.

### Precision lock
A concentrated event directed toward Enka's current or expected location.

Exact percentages and schedules remain undefined.

## 7. Precision failure

If precise location is unavailable, a broader fictional beam may occur.

This can support fictional stories involving:
- confused perception;
- unusual local experiences;
- mistaken identity;
- strange environmental anomalies.

### Editorial safety rule
Never use EduBeam to explain real-world missing persons, deaths, disasters or crimes as though the fiction were factual.

## 8. Effect progression

Possible fictional effects:
- enhanced recall;
- perceptual distortion;
- dual-location sensation;
- lucid/dream-like states;
- temporary displacement;
- partial materialisation;
- full materialisation;
- phasing;
- unusual awareness.

Effects vary by event and Enka's condition.

## 9. Central rule

> **EduBeam = high power / low control**

> **Awareness practice = lower power / higher control**

This contrast drives the long-term arc.

## 10. S00E01 implementation

E01 reconstructs the established 9 July 2026 Singapore event before explaining it.

The audience experiences the phenomenon first.

The archive labels evidence separately from interpretation.
