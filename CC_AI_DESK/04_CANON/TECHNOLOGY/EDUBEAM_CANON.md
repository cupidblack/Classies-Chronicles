# EduBeam Technology Canon

**Canon ID:** TECH-EDUBEAM  
**Version:** 0.5.0  
**Status:** WORKING CANON — FOUNDATION PHASE

## 1. Concept

EduBeam is an experimental fictional learning/neurological technology in the Chronicles universe.

Earlier archives establish:

- suspicious RF activity
- academic/perceptual anomalies
- unusual observation around Blue Crown
- the established 9 July 2026 Singapore event

EduBeam mechanics are fictional story logic, not real-world scientific claims.

## 2. Canon boundary

The following remain unknown unless supported by approved archive evidence:

- creator
- ownership
- engineering design
- actual purpose
- controller
- ultimate objective

## 3. Proposed targeting hierarchy

1. active network/location signal
2. recent network activity
3. last-known digital transaction
4. expected geographic location
5. broader-area targeting when precise targeting fails

This remains PROPOSED_NEW_CANON.

## 4. Cognitive targeting

A fictional neural/brainwave signature associated with Enka may later explain why the system can distinguish him from others.

This is fictional technology.

## 5. Event classes

### Routine/random scan
Low-intensity event with subtle or temporary effects.

### Processing-cycle event
Stronger event associated with major processing periods.

### Precision lock
Concentrated event directed toward Enka's current or expected location.

Do not hard-code percentages without producer-approved story evidence.

## 6. Precision failure

If precise location is unavailable, a broader-area fictional beam may occur.

**Editorial safety rule:** never use this mechanism to explain real-world missing persons, deaths, disasters or crimes.

## 7. Effect progression

Possible effects:

- enhanced recall
- perceptual distortion
- dual-location sensation
- lucid/dream-like states
- temporary displacement
- partial materialization
- full materialization
- phasing
- unusual awareness

Effects vary by event and Enka's condition.

## 8. Central rule

**EduBeam = high power / low control**  
**Awareness practice = lower power / higher control**

This contrast drives the long-term arc.
