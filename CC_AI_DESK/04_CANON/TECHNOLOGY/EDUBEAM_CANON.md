## 1. Concept

EduBeam is an experimental **fictional** learning/neurological technology in the Chronicles universe.

Earlier archives establish suspicious RF activity, academic/perceptual anomalies, unusual observation around Blue Crown and the established 9 July 2026 Singapore event.

EduBeam mechanics are fictional story logic and are not real-world scientific claims.

## 2. Established narrative facts

- The EduBeam is associated with a satellite/remote-beam phenomenon.
- Enka has experienced it before the Foundation Phase.
- The 9 July 2026 Singapore event is an established archive event.
- Strong exposure can produce abnormal perception and displacement-like effects.
- Effects dissipate rather than remaining permanently at maximum intensity.

## 3. Unknowns

Unless approved archive evidence establishes otherwise, these remain unknown:

- actual purpose;
- controller/operator;
- ultimate objective;
- whether the system is adaptive or experimental;
- why Enka is affected.

## 6. Event classes

### Routine/random scan
Low-intensity event with subtle or temporary effects.

### Processing-cycle event
Potentially stronger event associated with major processing periods.

### Precision lock
A concentrated event directed toward Enka's current or expected location.

Exact percentages, frequency and duration remain undefined unless approved story evidence requires them.

## 7. Precision failure and collateral effects

If precise location is unavailable, a broader fictional beam may occur. This can support stories involving confused perception or unusual local events.

**Editorial safety rule:** never use this fictional mechanism to explain real-world missing persons, deaths, disasters or crimes as though the fiction were factual.

## 8. Effect progression

Possible fictional effects include:

- enhanced recall;
- perceptual distortion;
- dual-location sensation;
- lucid/dream-like states;
- temporary displacement;
- partial materialization;
- full materialization;
- phasing;
- unusual awareness.

Effects vary by event and Enka's condition.

## 9. Central rule

This contrast drives the long-term arc.
