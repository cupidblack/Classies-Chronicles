## Important distinction

The AI Desk Architecture v22.0 has been reviewed but has **not yet been physically applied to the studio**.

Therefore E01 should use an incremental application approach.

## E01 minimum AI Desk layer

### 1. Role
AI acts as:
- co-host when invited;
- continuity checker;
- evidence/source assistant;
- question generator;
- timing/transition assistant.

### 2. Not the authority
The AI must not:
- become the narrator of all canon;
- decide unresolved story facts;
- fabricate news;
- publish autonomously.

### 3. Communication behaviour
Use the already-working three-way communication system.

Recommended operating states:
- LISTEN;
- RESPOND;
- PRODUCER ASSIST;
- MUTE / HOLD.

### 4. Recording discipline
Before the main recording:
- perform a short three-way test;
- verify independent AI mute;
- verify host-to-AI and AI-to-host levels;
- verify no feedback;
- record the test result.

## Future AI Desk architecture work

After E01 production stabilizes, progressively implement:
- source ledger;
- canon lookup;
- episode-state tracking;
- prompt assembly;
- QA report generation;
- archive indexing.

Do not rebuild the working communications chain merely to implement the narrative architecture.
