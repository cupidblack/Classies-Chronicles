# S00E01 — Foundations & Frequencies

## Main pilot topic

**How do we build a trustworthy foundation when the information, technology and experiences shaping us are not yet fully understood?**

### Story engine

Enka is not introduced as a finished superhero. He is introduced as a technically curious person trying to make sense of an established anomalous event. The 9 July 2026 Singapore EduBeam incident becomes the first major cinematic mystery of the Foundation Phase.

The episode then makes a deliberate transition:

**signal → evidence → uncertainty → learning → community → new mystery**

This mirrors the Foundation Phase itself: before the Chronicles officially begin, the audience learns what the platform is, why the archive matters, how stories are built, and why participation matters.

## Why this works as the first pilot

- It starts with a strong mystery without requiring a complete origin story.
- It gives returning viewers continuity while remaining accessible to new viewers.
- It introduces Buzzjuice Network and Buzzjuice Courses naturally.
- It establishes the Bible/news/education/story integration model.
- It demonstrates the AI Desk without making the AI the protagonist.
- It leaves enough unanswered questions to drive S00E02 and beyond.

## Canon boundary

The episode may establish the experience of the 9 July event and the visual phenomenon of displacement/materialization. It must not definitively explain who operates EduBeam, why Enka is targeted, or the complete physics of the abilities.
