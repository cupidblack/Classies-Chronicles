# News Research Protocol — S00E01

## Requested window

9 August 2026 through 17 October 2026.

## Reality constraint

As of 31 August 2026, 1 September–17 October is future time. Those headlines must not be invented.

## Research order

Adenta → Greater Accra → Ghana → West Africa → Bugis/Singapore → Singapore → Genting → Kuala Lumpur → collective summary → global awareness.

## Story-use rule

When a verified headline is selected, record:

- publication date;
- publisher;
- headline/title;
- location;
- source URL;
- why it matters to the episode;
- whether it is used as factual context or fictional inspiration.

## Fiction boundary

The Chronicle can say “in our fictional story, Cu might have…” but must not imply that a real person/event was actually caused or solved by Cupid.
