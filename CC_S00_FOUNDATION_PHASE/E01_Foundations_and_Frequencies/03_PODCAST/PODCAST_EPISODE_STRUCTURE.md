# S00E01 Podcast Structure — Foundations & Frequencies

**Target:** 35–45 minutes  
**Chronicle-first rule:** The ~200-second Chronicle must be approved before the podcast is treated as final programme content.

## Proposed running order

1. **Cold Open / Signal** — 0:00–0:15
2. **Jingle + Welcome** — 0:15–0:45
3. **Bible Reflection: Nehemiah 1–7** — 0:45–1:30
4. **Episode Overview** — 1:30–4:00
5. **Buzzjuice/Koware Introduction** — 4:00–7:00
6. **Verified Headlines / Awareness** — 7:00–12:00
7. **Chronicle Trailer** — ~20 seconds
8. **Main Discussion** — 12:20–27:00
9. **Chronicles of Cupid Black** — ~200 seconds
10. **Chronicle Discussion** — 30:20–36:00
11. **Community / Participation / Crowdfunding** — 36:00–40:00
12. **Next Episode Preview** — 40:00–42:00
13. **Closing Mystery** — 42:00–43:00
14. **Credits / Community Shorts** — optional

The exact timings may flex to preserve natural conversation.

## AI Desk participation

The AI should be silent until cued. It may contribute:

- a concise source clarification;
- a continuity observation;
- a question for the host;
- a clearly labelled interpretation;
- a short closing reflection.

It should not interrupt the host or guest.

## Guest policy

Guests are optional in S00E01. The pilot remains viable with host + AI co-host only.
