# S00E01 Visual & Audio Plan

## Visual language

- Archive interface: clean, investigative, documentary-like.
- Blue Crown flashbacks: warm archival texture.
- Singapore reconstruction: realistic contemporary urban environment.
- EduBeam: restrained light flicker, low-frequency atmosphere and perceptual distortion.
- Materialization: subtle dimming → shadow-like form → colour stabilization.
- Do not use excessive superhero effects.

## Suggested Chronicle scenes

1. Archive file / waveform
2. Blue Crown / RF scanner flashback
3. Bugis apartment packing
4. Hum and flickering lights
5. Enka leaving the building
6. Perceptual overlap / car door slapstick beat
7. Shadow-like materialization near colleague's apartment
8. Present-day AI Desk / new pulse / cut to black

## Audio

- Low, restrained EduBeam hum.
- Brief electrical flicker/static.
- Room and street ambience.
- Car-door impact for the comedic beat.
- Short silence immediately after the impact.
- Clean AI Desk confirmation tone.
- Final electronic pulse.

## Production principle

The sound design should make the event feel physical without claiming real-world scientific validity.
