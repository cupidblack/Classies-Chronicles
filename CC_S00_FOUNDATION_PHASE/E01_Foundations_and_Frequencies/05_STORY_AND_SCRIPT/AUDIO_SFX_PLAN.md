# Audio & SFX Plan — S00E01

## Core cues

- archive interface tone;
- low fictional EduBeam hum;
- subtle light/electronics flicker;
- car door impact;
- brief silence after the joke;
- spatial/phase transition sound;
- phone ring;
- final electronic pulse.

## Mixing principle

Narration remains intelligible above all effects. The EduBeam hum should feel physical and mysterious rather than painfully loud.

## Safety

No sound design should imply that a real consumer device can reproduce the fictional EduBeam.
