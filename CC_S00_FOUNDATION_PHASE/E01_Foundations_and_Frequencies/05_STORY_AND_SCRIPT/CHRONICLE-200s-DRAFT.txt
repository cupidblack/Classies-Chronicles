CLASSIES CHRONICLES
S00E01 — FOUNDATIONS & FREQUENCIES
CHRONICLE: THE SIGNAL BEFORE THE STORY
TARGET: ~200 SECONDS

[SFX: low room tone, keyboard, faint electronic hum]

NARRATOR:

Every story has a beginning.

But sometimes the beginning is not where you think it is.

On the Foundation Phase archive, one file keeps pulling our attention.

09 July 2026.

Singapore.

ANOMALOUS EVENT.

A waveform fills the screen.

[BEAT]

Then the waveform changes.

HOST:

That wasn't in the original file.

AI DESK:

Insufficient evidence.

HOST:

That's a very AI answer.

[CUT TO BLACK]

NARRATOR:

Before the signal, there were clues.

Blue Crown.

An old RF scanner.

Missing and inconsistent records.

Dreams that felt more like memories.

And a question Enka could never quite put down:

Was somebody watching?

[CUT: Singapore, 9 July 2026]

Enka was packing the last of his things in Bugis.

He was tired. Alert. Ready to leave.

Then he heard it.

A low hum.

The lights flickered.

He checked the signal and backed toward the car.

One step.

Two.

THUD.

He had backed straight into the car door.

ENKA:

If science wants my attention, it could at least knock.

[HUM INTENSIFIES]

NARRATOR:

Then the world changed.

The corridor was still there.

The car was still there.

And somehow, another place was there too.

For a moment, Enka could not tell which location was real.

The signal grew louder.

The light became almost unbearable.

Then—

silence.

[CUT: outside colleague's apartment]

The room seemed to darken.

Not completely.

Just enough to make the shadows look wrong.

A darker shape gathered.

Like dust passing through sunlight.

Like a person trying to remember how to become solid.

Colour slowly returned.

Enka appeared.

He looked around.

ENKA:

Where am I?

NARRATOR:

He had no answer.

No map could explain it.

No ordinary journey had brought him there.

And whatever had happened, he had not controlled it.

[CUT BACK: PRESENT-DAY ARCHIVE]

The host freezes the frame.

HOST:

So that's the origin?

AI DESK:

Insufficient evidence.

HOST:

You really enjoy that phrase.

AI DESK:

It is useful.

[A SMALL LAUGH]

NARRATOR:

Maybe that is the first lesson.

Perception is not understanding.

A signal is not an explanation.

And power without control is not freedom.

Nehemiah heard about broken walls, investigated what was real, organized a response, rebuilt — and stayed watchful.

Perhaps that is where our Foundation Phase begins too.

Not with answers.

With attention.

[CUT: waveform]

The room goes quiet.

A new pulse appears on the screen.

Nobody touches the file.

HOST:

Did you see that?

AI DESK:

Yes.

[BEAT]

NARRATOR:

The event was recorded on 9 July.

But something has just happened again.

[LOW HUM]

And this time...

the archive is watching back.

[MUSIC: short unresolved sting]

END
