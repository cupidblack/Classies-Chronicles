# Chronicles of Cupid Black — "The Signal Before the Story"
## S00E01 Chronicle — approximately 200 seconds
## Draft status: PRODUCER REVIEW PENDING

[0–18s — COLD OPEN: THE ARCHIVE]

VISUAL: A clean, dark archive interface.

A record opens:

09 JULY 2026 — SINGAPORE

A waveform pulses.

AI DESK:
"Archive record found. Event classification: confirmed. Cause: unknown."

NARRATOR:
"Some stories begin with a person. This one begins with a signal."

[18–43s — THE OLD CLUES]

FLASHES:
Blue Crown.
An RF scanner.
Old handwritten notes.
A sleeping Enka suddenly turning toward a sound.
A distant light.

NARRATOR:
"For years there had been strange records, unusual recall, dreams and signals nobody could quite explain."

Beat.

"A clue is not an explanation."

[43–82s — BUGIS, 9 JULY 2026]

Enka is packing the last bags from his Bugis apartment.

He checks the room.

One last bag.

A low HUM begins.

The lights flicker.

Enka freezes.

ENKA:
"Not again."

He grabs his scanner and moves toward the car.

The hum grows stronger.

He tries to keep the scanner pointed toward the disturbance while moving.

Then the EduBeam strikes.

[82–116s — THE OVERLAP]

The world seems to double.

For an instant Enka sees the car and another apartment at the same time.

He turns.

The hallway appears where the car should be.

He turns again.

The car is back.

He steps backward while staring at the scanner.

THUD.

He hits the car door.

Silence.

ENKA:
"If science wants my attention, it could at least knock."

He rubs his side.

The humour disappears as the image bends again.

[116–157s — MATERIALISATION]

CUT TO:

A quiet area near a colleague's apartment.

Nothing seems unusual.

Then the light subtly dims.

A faint shadow gathers at the edge of the frame.

Dust-like edges.

A human outline.

The figure becomes darker.

Then colour begins returning.

Enka stabilises.

He looks around.

ENKA:
"Where am I?"

His phone rings.

VOICE:
"Where are you?"

Enka looks around again.

ENKA:
"I... don't know."

[157–181s — THE QUESTION]

NARRATOR:
"The strange part wasn't simply that Enka had moved."

Pause.

"The strange part was remembering another place at the same time."

FLASH:
Blue Crown.

FLASH:
RF scanner.

FLASH:
Distant light.

NARRATOR:
"Technology? Neurology? Something else?"

Enka stares at his hands.

ENKA:
"I don't even know what is real anymore."

[181–200s — FOUNDATION / NEW SIGNAL]

RETURN TO THE PRESENT-DAY ARCHIVE.

The interface changes.

The Foundation Phase files appear:

COMMUNITY.
LEARNING.
PRODUCTION.
PARTICIPATION.

AI DESK:
"New signal detected."

The waveform pulses again.

AI DESK:
"Insufficient evidence."

HOST:
"Then why is the signal back?"

CUT TO BLACK.

FINAL ELECTRONIC PULSE.
