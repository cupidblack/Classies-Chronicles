# Chronicles of Cupid Black — “The Signal Before the Story”
## Approximately 200-second Chronicle draft

### 0–20s — THE FILE
Visual: dark archive interface. A dated record appears:

“09 JULY 2026 — SINGAPORE”

Narrator:
“Some stories begin with a person. This one begins with a signal.”

A waveform pulses.

### 20–48s — THE OLD CLUES
Rapid archival flashes:
Blue Crown. Exam papers. A blank record. An RF scanner. A sleeping Enka turning suddenly toward a sound.

Narrator:
“Years earlier there had been odd records, strange dreams, unusual recall and signals no one could quite explain.”

Beat.

“Back then, Enka thought he was investigating the mystery.”

### 48–90s — BUGIS, 9 JULY 2026
Enka packs his last bags.

A low hum.

The lights flicker.

He stops.

“Not again.”

He exits the building and gets into the car.

The hum intensifies.

The EduBeam strikes.

### 90–118s — THE SPLIT
Visual distortion.

Enka sees the car and another apartment overlaid.

His breathing changes.

He reaches for the scanner/phone while backing away.

He bumps into the car door.

Pause.

Enka:
“If science wants my attention, it could at least knock.”

The visual world bends again.

### 118–155s — MATERIALIZATION
Cut to the quiet area near the colleague's apartment.

A room appears normal.

Then slightly darker.

A shadow gathers.

Dust-like edges.

A human outline.

Colour slowly returns.

Enka fully materializes.

He looks around.

“Where am I?”

His phone rings.

The voice from Singapore asks where he is.

Enka looks at the unfamiliar surroundings.

“I... don't know.”

### 155–182s — THE QUESTION
Narrator:
“The strange part wasn't that he had moved.”

Pause.

“The strange part was that he remembered being somewhere else at the same time.”

Flash: Blue Crown.

Flash: RF scanner.

Flash: satellite-like light.

Narrator:
“Was it technology? A neurological effect? Something else?”

Enka:
“I don't even know what is real anymore.”

### 182–200s — THE NEW SIGNAL
Return to present-day archive.

The waveform changes.

A new pulse appears.

The AI Desk displays:

“INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE.”

Host:
“Then why is the signal back?”

Cut to black.

One final electronic pulse.
