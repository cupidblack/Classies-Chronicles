TITLE: Chronicles of Cupid Black — The Signal Before the Story

[0:00–0:20 — PRESENT-DAY ARCHIVE]
NARRATOR:
Some stories begin with a hero.
Others begin with a question.
This one begins with a signal.

A record from Singapore.
9 July 2026.

[0:20–0:48 — BUGIS, SINGAPORE]
Enka is packing the last of his things.

He has already had a strange day. Too many questions. Too many people acting strangely.

Then—

A hum.

He stops.

The lights flicker.

ENKA:
"Not again."

[0:48–1:12 — THE CORRIDOR]
The hum grows louder.

Enka moves toward the exit.

The sound follows him.

He recognizes it.

EDUBEAM.

He gets into the car, breathing hard.

For one second, everything becomes unnaturally clear.

Then the world seems to split.

[1:12–1:30 — COMEDY / IMPACT]
Enka turns too quickly, nearly clips the doorframe, catches himself and looks back at the door.

ENKA:
"If science wants my attention, it could at least knock."

Then the light changes.

The beam hits.

CUT TO WHITE.

[1:30–1:55 — PERCEPTUAL DISRUPTION]
No ordinary sense of time.

The car.
The corridor.
Another place.

All seem to overlap.

Enka reaches toward something he cannot identify.

ENKA:
"I don't even know what is real anymore."

The image collapses.

[1:55–2:18 — MATERIALIZATION]
Silence.

A quiet apartment area near a university campus.

A faint shadow-like form appears.

Edges first.

Then outline.

Then colour.

Enka is suddenly there.

He looks around.

ENKA:
"Where am I?"

His phone rings.

He stares at it.

[2:18–2:48 — THE OLDER SIGNAL]
NARRATOR:
The strange part was not that Enka had experienced something unusual.

The strange part was that the archive had been whispering about unusual things for years.

Missing records.

Unexplained recall.

Dreams that felt too real.

RF signals that should not have been there.

A young man who kept asking questions nobody seemed ready to answer.

[2:48–3:05 — THE PATTERN]
NARRATOR:
Maybe July 2026 was not the beginning.

Maybe it was the moment the old mystery became impossible to ignore.

Enka looks toward the sky.

He does not see the source.

But he feels it.

[3:05–3:20 — HOOK]
A faint electronic tone.

The present-day archive monitor registers a signal.

NARRATOR:
The first signal may have been recorded years ago.

The problem is—

it may not be finished.
