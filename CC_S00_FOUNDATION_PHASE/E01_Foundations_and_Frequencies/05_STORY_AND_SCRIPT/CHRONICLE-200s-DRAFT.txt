# Chronicles of Cupid Black — “The Signal Before the Story”
## S00E01 Chronicle — producer-ready draft, target approximately 200 seconds

[0–18s — COLD OPEN: THE ARCHIVE]

VISUAL: Present-day archive interface. A waveform pulses.

ON SCREEN:
09 JULY 2026 — SINGAPORE
ARCHIVAL RECORD — CONFIRMED
CAUSE — UNKNOWN

AI DESK:
“Archive record found. Event classification: confirmed. Cause: unknown.”

NARRATOR:
“Some stories begin with a person. This one begins with a signal.”

[18–43s — OLD CLUES]

FLASHES: Blue Crown. RF scanner. handwritten notes. Enka turning in his sleep toward a distant sound. A pale light beyond a window.

NARRATOR:
“For a long time, there were clues. Strange records. Unusual recall. Dreams. Signals nobody could quite explain.”

BEAT.

“A clue is not an explanation.”

[43–82s — BUGIS, 9 JULY 2026]

Enka packs the final bags in his Bugis apartment. He checks the room twice.

ENKA:
“One more bag. Then I'm done.”

A LOW HUM begins.

The lights flicker.

Enka freezes.

ENKA:
“Not again.”

He grabs the scanner and moves toward the car, trying to keep the device pointed toward the disturbance.

The hum deepens.

The EduBeam strikes.

[82–116s — THE OVERLAP]

The world doubles.

For an instant Enka sees the car and another apartment at once. The hallway seems to fold into the street.

He turns.

The car is there.

He turns again.

The hallway is there.

He steps backward while staring at the scanner.

THUD.

He hits the car door.

Silence.

ENKA:
“If science wants my attention, it could at least knock.”

A tiny breath of a laugh.

Then the humour disappears as the scene bends again.

[116–157s — MATERIALISATION]

CUT TO: A quiet area near a colleague's apartment.

The light subtly dims, as if a cloud has passed over the room.

A faint darkening gathers at the edge of frame.

Dust-like edges.

A human outline.

The figure is initially darker than the room.

Colour gradually returns.

Enka stabilises.

He looks around.

ENKA:
“Where am I?”

His phone rings.

VOICE:
“Where are you?”

Enka looks around again.

ENKA:
“I... don't know.”

[157–181s — THE QUESTION]

NARRATOR:
“The strange part wasn't simply that Enka had moved.”

PAUSE.

“It was remembering another place at the same time.”

FLASH: Blue Crown.
FLASH: RF scanner.
FLASH: Distant light.

NARRATOR:
“Technology? Neurology? Something else?”

Enka stares at his hands.

ENKA:
“I don't even know what is real anymore.”

[181–200s — FOUNDATION / NEW SIGNAL]

RETURN TO PRESENT-DAY ARCHIVE.

FILES appear:
COMMUNITY.
LEARNING.
PRODUCTION.
PARTICIPATION.

AI DESK:
“New signal detected.”

The waveform pulses.

AI DESK:
“Insufficient evidence.”

HOST:
“Then why is the signal back?”

CUT TO BLACK.

FINAL ELECTRONIC PULSE.
