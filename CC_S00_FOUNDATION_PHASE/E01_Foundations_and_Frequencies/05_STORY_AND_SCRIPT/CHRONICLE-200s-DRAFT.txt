# Chronicles of Cupid Black — “The Signal Before the Story”
## S00E01 Chronicle — approximately 200 seconds

[0–20s — THE FILE]

VISUAL: A dark but clean archive interface. A record appears:

09 JULY 2026 — SINGAPORE

NARRATOR:
“Some stories begin with a person. This one begins with a signal.”

A waveform pulses.

[20–48s — THE OLD CLUES]

FLASHES: Blue Crown. An RF scanner. Old notes. A sleeping Enka suddenly turning toward a sound.

NARRATOR:
“For years there had been odd records, strange dreams, unusual recall... and signals no one could quite explain.”

Beat.

“Back then, Enka thought he was investigating the mystery.”

[48–92s — BUGIS, 9 JULY 2026]

Enka packs the last bags from his Bugis apartment.

A low hum begins.

The lights flicker.

ENKA:
“Not again.”

He moves outside toward the car.

The hum intensifies.

The EduBeam strikes.

[92–122s — THE OVERLAP]

Reality seems to double.

Enka sees the car... and another apartment... occupying the same moment.

He tries to keep his scanner/phone pointed toward the signal while backing away.

THUD.

He bumps into the car door.

Silence.

ENKA:
“If science wants my attention, it could at least knock.”

He rubs his side.

The joke disappears into silence as the visual world bends again.

[122–160s — MATERIALIZATION]

CUT TO a quiet area near a colleague's apartment.

The scene is normal.

Then it becomes subtly darker.

A shadow-like form gathers at the edge of the frame.

Dust-like edges.

A human outline.

Colour slowly returns.

Enka stabilizes.

He looks around.

ENKA:
“Where am I?”

His phone rings.

VOICE:
“Where are you?”

Enka looks around again.

ENKA:
“I... don't know.”

[160–183s — THE QUESTION]

NARRATOR:
“The strange part wasn't simply that Enka had moved.”

Pause.

“The strange part was remembering another place at the same time.”

FLASH: Blue Crown.

FLASH: RF scanner.

FLASH: a distant light over the night sky.

NARRATOR:
“Technology? Neurology? Something else?”

ENKA:
“I don't even know what is real anymore.”

[183–200s — THE FOUNDATION]

RETURN TO the present-day archive.

The waveform changes.

A new pulse appears.

AI DESK:
“Insufficient evidence.”

HOST:
“Then why is the signal back?”

CUT TO BLACK.

FINAL ELECTRONIC PULSE.
