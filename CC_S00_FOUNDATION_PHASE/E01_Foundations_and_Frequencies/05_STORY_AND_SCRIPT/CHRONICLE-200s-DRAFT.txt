TITLE: Chronicles of Cupid Black — The Signal Before the Story

[0:00–0:18 — ARCHIVE HUM / NARRATOR]

Some stories begin with a person.

Others begin with a question.

This one may have begun with a signal hiding inside an old school record.

[0:18–0:48 — BLUE CROWN, 2000]

Cupid Enka was trying to finish his studies, keep his relationships from becoming a full-time occupation, and understand why his own mind sometimes seemed to be several pages ahead of him.

Some days, answers arrived before he remembered studying them.

Other days, familiar subjects became blank walls.

Then came the records.

A missing module.

A result that did not seem to match his memory.

And a question nobody seemed interested in answering.

[0:48–1:15 — RF INVESTIGATION]

Enka noticed something else.

A hum.

A strange signal.

Towers that were supposed to be ordinary communications equipment.

Frequencies that did not behave like ordinary school equipment.

So he did what any sensible electronics enthusiast would do.

He built an RF scanner.

Which was, naturally, when sensible behaviour left the building.

[1:15–1:32 — SLAPSTICK]

He followed the signal.

The meter climbed.

Enka stepped backward without looking.

BANG.

Door.

Pride: injured.

RF scanner: still working.

Enka: considerably less impressed with science.

[1:32–1:55 — THE SIGNAL]

But the signal was real.

Or at least, something was real.

He began keeping records.

Dreams.

Missing time.

Sudden flashes of information.

Places that felt familiar before he had ever seen them.

And the uncomfortable possibility that the experiment was not something he was studying.

Maybe he was part of it.

[1:55–2:22 — PERCEPTUAL SPLIT]

Then, one night, the signal changed.

For a fraction of a second, Enka saw two places at once.

One was where he stood.

The other was somewhere he could not explain.

The room seemed to darken at its edges.

He blinked.

The second place vanished.

No proof.

No witness.

Only the feeling that reality had briefly opened a second door.

Enka stared into the darkness.

“I don't even know what is real anymore.”

[2:22–2:55 — ARCHIVE / PRESENT DAY]

The old records tell us that Enka was being observed.

They tell us that his response to the technology was unusual.

They tell us that other students were affected differently.

But they do not tell us everything.

Years later, another name would begin to follow him.

Cupid Black.

But that name belongs to a later chapter.

For now, there is only a student, a signal, a mystery—

and an archive that may have recorded the beginning of something much bigger.

[2:55–3:20 — PRESENT-DAY HOOK]

Today, we know that education and technology are moving closer together.

The real world is asking how much capability is useful, how much is responsible, and who should control it.

In our Chronicle, Enka is still asking the same question.

The signal has not disappeared.

It has only learned to wait.
