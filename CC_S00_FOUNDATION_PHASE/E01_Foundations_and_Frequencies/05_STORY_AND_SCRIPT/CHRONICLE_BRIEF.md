# Chronicle Brief — S00E01

**Title:** The Signal Before the Story  
**Series:** Classies Chronicles  
**Season:** S00 — The Foundation Phase  
**Target:** ~200 seconds  
**Narrative role:** Continuity bridge / mystery ignition

## Logline

While reviewing the archive for the Foundation Phase, the production team discovers a strange change in a file documenting Enka's established 9 July 2026 EduBeam incident. The story reconstructs the event without explaining it — and ends when the archive produces a new signal after the incident is supposedly over.

## Audience promise

By the end of the Chronicle, viewers should want to know:

1. What exactly happened to Enka?
2. Why does the archive appear to be changing?
3. Who — or what — is still watching?
4. What will Enka learn next?

## Tone

- grounded
- mysterious
- cinematic
- lightly funny
- human rather than superheroic

## Visual language

Start with clean archive/production visuals.

Move into warmer historical imagery.

During the EduBeam event, introduce:

- signal distortion
- flickering light
- overlapping images
- subtle shadow/dimming
- controlled visual disorientation

Avoid excessive superhero effects. The phenomenon should feel unsettling because it is almost believable.

## Key production constraint

The Chronicle is approximately 200 seconds.

Target spoken narration: **480–530 words**, depending on narrator pace.

The earlier proposal of 2,400–2,600 words is rejected because it would require roughly 15–18 minutes of narration, not 200 seconds.
