# Chronicle Brief — S00E01

**Title:** The Signal Before the Story  
**Target:** ~200 seconds  
**Tone:** mystery, grounded fictional sci-fi, reflective, lightly humorous  
**POV:** close third-person / Enka-focused

## Central question
What happens when the signal is real but its meaning is not yet understood?

## Thematic bridge
Nehemiah's rebuilding motif mirrors the Foundation Phase: inspect the broken pieces, gather reliable information, assign responsibility and build before scaling.

## Visual language
Archive UI; restrained night blues/greys; waveform; subtle flicker; realistic Singapore environments; subtle dimming; shadow/dust materialisation; colour restoration; no superhero costume; no giant energy blast.

## Audio
Low fictional hum; electrical ambience; heartbeat; one car-door impact; silence after the joke; final electronic pulse.

## Canon discipline
The audience sees the event before the story explains it. The Chronicle ends with a question rather than a power reveal.
