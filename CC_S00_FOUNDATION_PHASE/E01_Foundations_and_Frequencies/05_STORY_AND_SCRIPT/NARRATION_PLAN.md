# S00E01 Narration Plan

## Voices
1. Human voice actor — primary Chronicle narrator
2. Enka voice — character dialogue
3. AI voice — controlled AI Desk / co-host
4. Producer voice — podcast host

## Human review
The producer reviews all narration audio before lock.

## Recording rule
Do not treat the AI voice as an authority on fictional canon. The AI Desk's dialogue should sound precise but appropriately uncertain.

## Performance direction
Narrator:
- calm;
- cinematic;
- curious;
- restrained.

Enka:
- natural;
- slightly stressed;
- dry humour;
- not melodramatic.

AI Desk:
- concise;
- neutral;
- analytical;
- occasionally surprising because it flags uncertainty rather than pretending to know.
