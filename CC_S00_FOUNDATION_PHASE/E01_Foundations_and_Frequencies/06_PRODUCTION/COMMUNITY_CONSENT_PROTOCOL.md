# Community Participation & Consent Protocol

1. A member chooses whether Chronicle participation is enabled.
2. Consent is stored as an explicit profile/platform state.
3. Only consenting members enter the active Chronicle retrieval set.
4. If consent is withdrawn, the member is removed from active Chronicle processing.
5. AI must not infer consent from ordinary course participation.
6. Final publication requires a separate human review for privacy, licensing and context.
7. Members can contribute story ideas, comments or experiences without automatically becoming fictional characters.

The goal is to make participation opt-in, reversible and auditable.
