# S00E01 Decision Log

## D001 — Pilot is a mystery doorway
**Decision:** Use "The Signal Before the Story" rather than a complete superhero origin.  
**Reason:** It preserves narrative runway and aligns with the established 9 July event.

## D002 — 9 July event is the principal anchor
**Decision:** Reconstruct the established Bugis/Singapore event rather than inventing a new August origin.  
**Reason:** Chapter 4 already supplies the strongest continuity anchor.

## D003 — No premature power mastery
**Decision:** E01 shows anomalous perception and involuntary displacement only.  
**Reason:** Later controlled materialization and advanced phenomena need progression.

## D004 — No exact power percentages
**Decision:** Remove rigid 10–30% or similar intensity claims from canon.  
**Reason:** They are not required for storytelling and create false precision.

## D005 — Course sequencing
**Decision:** E01 introduces Koware/Buzzjuice; registration/orientation and Health & Safety are later episodes.  
**Reason:** Matches producer sequencing and avoids overloading the pilot.

## D006 — News is optional for E01
**Decision:** Do not force a headline into the Chronicle.  
**Reason:** The opening mystery is stronger when the signal itself is the story.

## D007 — Future news is never fabricated
**Decision:** Dates after 2026-08-31 remain pending.  
**Reason:** The requested range extends into the future relative to package preparation.

## D008 — AI is a participant, not protagonist
**Decision:** Controlled co-host mode with a maximum of two substantive questions.  
**Reason:** Preserves human-led storytelling and mystery.
