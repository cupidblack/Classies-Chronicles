## Producer / Creative & Design Director

- final editorial authority;
- story approval;
- canon approval;
- AI participation control;
- publication approval.

## Podcast Developer / Media Producer

- recording coordination;
- timeline assembly;
- Chronicle/podcast integration;
- export and archive.

## Graphic Designer

- Chronicle visual assets;
- episode artwork;
- social/marketing graphics.

## Talent / PR / A&R

- narrator and guest coordination;
- contributor communication;
- release/consent coordination.

## Enterprise Architect

- AI Desk integration;
- data/consent architecture;
- automation and repository governance.

These assignments are functional responsibilities and should be mapped to the actual Koware organizational chart roles during operational staffing.
