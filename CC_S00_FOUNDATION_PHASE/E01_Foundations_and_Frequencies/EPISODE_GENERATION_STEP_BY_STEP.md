# Classies Chronicles — Standard Episode Generation Workflow v1.0

## Phase 0 — Create the episode shell
Create the episode folder and copy the canonical context templates.

## Phase 1 — Complete metadata
Fill episode, season, chapter, timeline, previous episode, producer and production status.

## Phase 2 — Assemble sources
Attach:
- Bible;
- course reference;
- current headlines;
- season notes;
- chapter notes;
- previous episode;
- character canon;
- technology/world canon;
- archive references.

## Phase 3 — Continuity preflight
The AI Desk uses `CURRENT_EPISODE` first, then only the producer-authorized broader scope.

Identify:
- chronology;
- established facts;
- unresolved questions;
- contradictions;
- character state;
- previous ending.

## Phase 4 — Story contract
Define central question, theme, emotional arc, conflict, turning point and ending hook.

## Phase 5 — News and Bible integration
Select factual material and thematic connections. Keep real-world reporting separate from fictional causality.

## Phase 6 — Generate story outline
Produce a scene/act outline. Do not yet generate final media.

## Phase 7 — Producer story gate
Producer reviews and approves/rejects proposed new canon and story direction.

## Phase 8 — Generate Chronicle package
Generate:
- brief;
- narrative;
- script;
- scene timings;
- visual prompts;
- audio/SFX;
- QA.

## Phase 9 — Chronicle production
Create artwork/animation, narration and sound design.

## Phase 10 — Chronicle approval
The approximately 200-second Chronicle becomes the approved narrative core.

## Phase 11 — Trailer
Extract the 20-second trailer from the approved Chronicle. Do not invent a separate trailer story.

## Phase 12 — Podcast construction
Build the podcast around the approved Chronicle.

Recommended order:
Cold Open → Jingle → Bible reflection → Overview → Koware/Buzzjuice → News → Trailer → Main Discussion → Chronicle → Discussion → Community/Crowdfunding → Preview → Mystery → Credits.

## Phase 13 — Record and edit
Use the commissioned communications architecture. Preserve independent AI audio control.

## Phase 14 — QA
Check:
- continuity;
- factual news;
- consent;
- licensing;
- timing;
- audio;
- captions;
- AI mode;
- Chronicle/podcast consistency.

## Phase 15 — Archive freeze
Store final episode-owned data. Update canon only through approved change records.

## Phase 16 — AI Desk post-episode update
Record observations. Generate persona proposals. Do not silently modify the permanent persona.

## Phase 17 — Re-index
Rebuild or update the derived search index from the authoritative archive.

## Phase 18 — Prepare next episode
Create the next episode shell using the same workflow.

### Governing principle
**Proven need → small implementation → test → measure → document → standardize → automate → scale.**
