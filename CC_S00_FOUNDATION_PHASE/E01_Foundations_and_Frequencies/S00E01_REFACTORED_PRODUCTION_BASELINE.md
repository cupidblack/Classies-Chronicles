## Main Topic

**Foundations & Frequencies — Origins, Signals and the Responsibility That Comes With Knowledge**

## Chronicle

**The Signal Before the Story**

The Chronicle is deliberately a mystery seed. It revisits established 2000 material and adds only a subtle perceptual split. It does not explain the full superhero system in the pilot.

## Why this is the best pilot story

It allows the audience to discover the franchise while the production team discovers the production system.

It introduces:

- education;
- technology;
- faith;
- current affairs;
- AI;
- community;
- the EduBeam mystery;
- the future Cupid Black arc.

The core question is:

> What happens when education, technology and knowledge give a person access to something they are not yet prepared to control?

## Important corrections applied from the GitHub review

1. The existing physical communications system is left unchanged because it is already working.
2. The AI Desk is introduced as a persistent knowledge/governance layer, not as a replacement audio system.
3. Cupid Black and EduBeam have separate authoritative canon files.
4. Historical 2000 Enka is not automatically called Cupid Black.
5. The drone reproducer and full superpower mechanics are future material.
6. EduBeam's exact creator and numerical intensity percentages are not invented as established facts.
7. Real news is never fabricated.
8. The selected headline is stored as factual source material and the fictional Chronicle is clearly separated from it.
9. The Bible passage is producer-verified rather than AI-selected.
10. The course reference is renamed to `03_COURSE_LESSON_REFERENCE.md`.
11. Episode-level prompt generation is separated from Part/Scene generation.
12. Producer approval remains the authority for new canon.
13. Chronicle approval is a hard gate before final podcast recording.
14. The trailer is derived from the approved Chronicle.
15. Retrieval references, consent, licensing, hashes and validation status are part of the episode record.
16. AI persona evolution remains proposal-based and versioned.
17. Season 00 automation remains assisted/manual first.

## Studio commissioning

Keep the working Pavilion/ENVY communications architecture governed by the specialist Podcast Communications Module.

Do not redesign it merely to accommodate the AI Desk.

Commission the AI Desk around the working studio.

## AI Desk commissioning

Start with:

- CURRENT_EPISODE retrieval;
- canon retrieval;
- controlled scope expansion;
- permission filtering;
- LISTEN;
- ASSIST;
- CO-HOST;
- independent MUTE;
- uncertainty handling;
- provenance logging.

Do not introduce autonomous publishing, vector search, multi-agent orchestration or automated community harvesting as prerequisites for S00E01.

## Acceptance philosophy

The pilot passes when the system is:

- narratively coherent;
- technically recordable;
- auditable;
- provenance-aware;
- consent-aware;
- licensing-aware;
- reproducible;
- understandable by the producer.

Perfection is not the goal.

A repeatable production cycle is.
