# Season 00 — Foundation Phase Production Guide

## Purpose

Season 00 prepares the audience, studio, AI Desk, community and production pipeline for the first official Classies Chronicles season.

## Suggested episode progression

- **S00E01:** Foundations & Frequencies — Koware/Buzzjuice introduction and the signal mystery.
- **S00E02:** Registration & Orientation — how participation begins.
- **S00E03:** Health & Safety Programme — programme overview and why it belongs in the Foundation Phase.
- **S00E04:** Specific lesson introduction — a first substantive lesson/story integration.

Additional episodes can cover Bible stories, news, blogcasts, crowdfunding, affiliate concepts, community participation and other FYI/need-to-know topics as appropriate.

## Production principle

Each episode can be informative while still feeding the Chronicles storyline. The story should remain entertaining rather than becoming a collection of advertisements.
