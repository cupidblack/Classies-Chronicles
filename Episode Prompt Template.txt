## 2. Issues & Improvement Opportunities Identified

### ISSUE 1 — Cognitive overload for reviewers

While powerful, **v1.65.62 is reviewer-heavy**:

* Some fields exist only to *control other fields*
* Some validation concepts are repeated across sections in different words

**Effect**: Harder onboarding, slower updates, higher error risk.

### ISSUE 2 — Duplicate intent across sections

Examples:

* `preflight_auto_fix_*`, `scene_suggestion_engine.auto_apply_policy`, and `placeholder_policy.auto_generation_policy`

  * All control *automatic intervention*
* `scene_tags_list`, `additional_recommended_tags`, `scene_suggestion_engine.suggestion_types`

  * All affect *scene annotation completeness*

These have different scopes but overlapping *intent*.

### ISSUE 3 — Educational & motif logic slightly fragmented

Educational content rules are spread across:

* Part-Level Variables
* Educational Content Handling
* Flashback/Motif rules
* QA metrics

**Effect**: Reviewers must “mentally assemble” the logic.

### ISSUE 4 — Missing explicit “criticality” model

You define:

* Priority levels
* Safety-Hardened behavior

But **what makes a field “critical” is implicit**, not explicit.

### ISSUE 5 — No single “Narrative Contract” summary

There is no **one place** that states:

> “This is what *must* exist in every Part for it to be considered valid.”

That forces reviewers to infer from multiple sections.

---

## 3. Design Goals for v1.65.63

For this increment, I targeted:

1. **Clarity without removing power**
2. **Explicit criticality & enforcement rules**
3. **Further merging of overlapping control mechanisms**
4. **Better reviewer ergonomics**
5. **Zero breaking changes**

## SECTION 1 — NARRATIVE CONTRACT CORE (New)

> **Purpose:** Defines the *minimum narrative guarantees* that every valid Part must satisfy, regardless of variant.

### Mandatory Narrative Elements

* **POV anchor** — A single dominant focal character.
* **Micro-conflict** — A small obstacle or puzzle that drives momentum.
* **Chapter continuity action** — Explicit advancement of chapter key factor.
* **Educational moment** — Diegetic learning interaction.
* **Theme encounter** — On-screen manifestation of episode theme.
* **Sensory grounding** — Multi-sensory description per scene.
* **Wardrobe/prop continuity** — Visual continuity anchors.
* **Verse or motif integration** — If supplied, must be meaningfully embedded.
* **Exactly one slapstick beat** — Harmless, non-disruptive humor.

Failure to meet any mandatory element:

* **Blocks generation** in Safety-Hardened mode.
* **Triggers auto-fix or warning** in other variants.
