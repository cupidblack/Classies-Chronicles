## Purpose

This package refactors the S00E01 material into a coherent, Chronicle-first production system. It preserves established Chapter 4 history, separates archival facts from proposed canon, integrates the Buzzjuice/Koware introduction without turning the Chronicle into an advertisement, and prepares the studio for controlled AI co-host participation.

## Key corrections from the prior package

1. The **9 July 2026 Singapore EduBeam event remains the principal continuity anchor**. It is not rewritten as a new origin event.
2. Unsupported exact dates such as a fixed **25 August 2026 Genting arrival** are removed from canon unless independently confirmed by the archive.
3. The claim that **2001 was definitively Enka's first materialization** is not treated as confirmed. Earlier anomalies remain archival material unless the archive explicitly establishes a materialization.
4. Exact EduBeam percentages, satellite-pass timing, sub-meter targeting accuracy and other hard technical specifications are not canonized without evidence.
5. The pilot does **not** reveal the complete mechanism of Enka's abilities.
6. Real news is used as a thematic mirror unless a fictional intervention is explicitly invented and clearly separated from real reporting.
7. The future portion of the requested headline window is never fabricated. The headline file records the research cutoff and pending period.
8. The Chronicle must be approved before the 20-second trailer is derived and before the final podcast is locked.
9. The AI Desk is a controlled co-host, not an autonomous narrator or publisher.
10. Dangerous real-world experimentation is never presented as actionable instruction.
