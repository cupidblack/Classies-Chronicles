## Key design decisions

### Decision 1 — Keep the pilot focused
The pilot uses the 9 July 2026 Singapore EduBeam event as the mystery doorway instead of attempting to narrate all of Chapter 4.

### Decision 2 — Evidence vs explanation
The AI Desk is used as an uncertainty classifier. This creates a believable narrative discipline and prevents the story from over-explaining the supernatural.

### Decision 3 — Delay superhero mastery
The pilot shows involuntary displacement and anomalous perception only. Controlled teleportation, phasing, telepathy, telekinesis and dimensional travel remain future possibilities.

### Decision 4 — One principal slapstick beat
The car-door collision provides light humour without undermining the seriousness of the event.

### Decision 5 — Foundation Phase is diegetic
Koware Group, Buzzjuice Network and Buzzjuice Courses are introduced as part of the rebuilding of the show's community and production foundation.

### Decision 6 — Future news is never fabricated
The requested headline window extends beyond 31 August 2026. Future items are therefore marked pending.

### Decision 7 — One canonical character location
Cupid Black characteristics belong in:
`CC_AI_DESK/04_CANON/CHARACTERS/CUPID_BLACK_CHARACTER_CANON.md`

### Decision 8 — Chronicle before trailer
The 20-second trailer is derived only after the approximately 200-second Chronicle is approved.

### Decision 9 — Member participation cannot buy canon
Course participation, subscription and crowdfunding can unlock approved participation opportunities, but do not allow members to override canon or editorial standards.

### Decision 10 — Fictional safety boundary
EduBeam is explicitly fictional. Future experiments are written as narrative devices, not as real-world instructions for dangerous electrical or neurological experimentation.
