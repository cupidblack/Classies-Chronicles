# S00E01 Step-by-Step Production Plan

## Gate 0 — Freeze the commissioning facts
- Episode ID: S00E01.
- Title: Foundations & Frequencies.
- Chronicle: The Signal Before the Story.
- Present-day framing: Foundation Phase.
- Historical reconstruction: 9 July 2026, Bugis/Singapore.
- Bible motif: Nehemiah 1–7.
- Course scope: Koware Group → Buzzjuice Network → Buzzjuice Courses introduction.
- No formal Health & Safety lesson in E01.

## Gate 1 — Prepare sources
Attach to the episode prompt:
1. current episode context;
2. Chapter 4 archive;
3. approved archive stories;
4. Cupid Black canon;
5. EduBeam canon;
6. course reference;
7. verified news snapshot;
8. previous-episode references when available.

If a source conflicts with canon, stop and flag it. Do not silently repair continuity.

## Gate 2 — Build the Chronicle first
Use the updated episode-generation contract. Produce:
- story brief;
- scene plan;
- 200-second narrated Chronicle;
- visual/SFX plan;
- provenance/QA report.

Producer locks the Chronicle before recording the podcast.

## Gate 3 — Derive the trailer
Cut a ~20-second trailer from the locked Chronicle. No trailer-only canon.

## Gate 4 — Build the podcast
Recommended sequence:
1. Cold open / signal.
2. Jingle + welcome.
3. 5–20 second Nehemiah reflection.
4. Episode topic: why the Foundation Phase exists.
5. Brief trailer.
6. Main discussion: Koware Group, Buzzjuice Network and Buzzjuice Courses.
7. Full Chronicle.
8. Open discussion: evidence, perception, technology and learning.
9. Community / participation / crowdfunding.
10. Next episode: registration & orientation.
11. Closing mystery.
12. Credits + approved community shorts.

## Gate 5 — Studio commissioning
- ENVY = master OBS/programme control.
- Pavilion = producer/communications/AI Desk.
- Keep AI return separate and muteable.
- Record a backup on Pavilion where practical.
- Use REAPER for stems; OBS for programme capture; CapCut for final video assembly if preferred.
- Keep 48 kHz / 16-bit as the project baseline where supported by the actual production path.

## Gate 6 — AI co-host test
Run in this order:
LISTEN → ASSIST → AUDIO → CO-HOST.

Verify:
- AI can hear permitted participants;
- AI does not hear its own return;
- AI can be muted immediately;
- guest can hear host + AI;
- host can hear guest + AI;
- AI can hear host + guest;
- AI cannot publish or override consent/licensing.

## Gate 7 — Human review
Producer reviews:
- continuity;
- tone;
- factual news separation;
- consent;
- licensing;
- narration;
- pronunciation;
- humour;
- visual continuity;
- final mix.

## Gate 8 — Archive and release
Create:
- final video;
- final audio;
- Chronicle master;
- trailer;
- transcript;
- show notes;
- provenance manifest;
- SHA-256 manifest.

Only after acceptance should the episode be published.
