# Foundation Phase Production Guide

## Recommended episode architecture

Every Foundation Phase episode should have two linked layers:

### Layer A — The Chronicle

A short, cinematic fictional story that integrates:

- one Bible/theme reference;
- one education/platform reference;
- selected real-world awareness;
- established Classies continuity;
- a clear character problem;
- one mystery or hook.

### Layer B — The Podcast

A human conversation that explains, debates and expands the ideas introduced by the Chronicle.

This keeps the show entertaining while allowing the podcast to carry context that would overload the short story.

## Recurring production loop

**Context → Research → Story → Chronicle → Trailer → Podcast → QA → Archive**

## Canon governance

The canon repository is the long-term memory of the series. Do not use an episode draft as a substitute for canon.

A new story detail becomes permanent only after it is classified and approved.

## Studio integration

The production package assumes the three-way communications system is already operational. The Classies repository should document the interface and control policy, but the studio routing implementation remains governed by the Koware Management studio files.

## Participation model

Members may influence future story participation through:

- course participation;
- subscription/community participation;
- crowdfunding tiers;
- approved contributions.

Participation should affect story opportunities without allowing contributors to bypass canon governance or safety rules.

## Lean launch strategy

For S00E01, do not make optional guests, large media packages or future headline windows blockers.

The minimum viable episode is:

1. Chronicle;
2. trailer;
3. host podcast;
4. controlled AI co-host;
5. Bible reflection;
6. Buzzjuice/Koware introduction;
7. verified awareness headlines;
8. next-episode hook.

## Future Foundation Phase sequence

A feasible progression is:

- **S00E01:** Foundations & Frequencies — what is this ecosystem and what happened?
- **S00E02:** Registration, Orientation & Entry — how does a person enter the ecosystem?
- **S00E03:** Health & Safety Program — what is the education system?
- **S00E04:** First substantive lesson / applied story — demonstrate the learning model.

The order may change if production or news makes another sequence more compelling.
